Camera calibration — ADR 0048 (pose) + 0012 (extrinsic)

Two one-time steps unlock 3D pose: (1) intrinsic from a printed ChArUco board, then (2) extrinsic from four marker cards taped to the floor at known corners. After both, every detected marker reports world XYZ + yaw/pitch/roll, and /track3d shows the scene in metres.

Step 1 — Intrinsic calibration (ChArUco board)

Print on A4 (or larger) and keep flat — a foam-board mount works best.
